# Common directing failures

- **Different texture, same world:** a new surface is not a new spatial or material
  relationship. Change what the subject can do or how layers interact.
- **Same performance in every passage:** entering, looking up, acting, cutting
  becomes predictable. Choose a different relationship for the next passage.
- **Unmotivated gesture:** be able to explain why this subject reacts here.
- **A late transition:** starting a growing mask on the cue makes its perceptual
  midpoint late. Place the chosen center on the cue, including its preparation.
- **Every attack gets the same response:** select meaningful anchors, let secondary
  details follow dense activity, and allow deliberate holds.
- **A successful render hides uncertain timing:** inspect query.timing and the
  corresponding window before committing to the grid. Support scores are not
  calibrated probabilities; verify cannot establish the correct BPM.
- **Stale edits:** saved stage boundaries and resolved added/moved/deleted cues
  govern authored responses. Do not also fire a moved cue at its old source time.
